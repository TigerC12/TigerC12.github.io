Teleoperated robotic hand

Glove lead

A group project to build a robotic hand driven by an operator wearing a DOGlove, the open-source haptic force-feedback glove from the TEA Lab. I was responsible for the glove. PCB · Embedded 2025

Badminton racket accelerometer

A custom data-acquisition board that mounts to a badminton racket and records 3-axis swing acceleration and impact force at 1000 Hz. I designed it independently in KiCad: an ESP32-WROOM-32E reading an MPU-6050 IMU over I²C, USB-C with a CH340C bridge for programming and data offload, ESD protection on the data lines, and a TP4056 LiPo charger feeding a 3.3 V regulator so it runs untethered. The layout keeps the antenna clear with a keep-out zone and puts the battery footprint under the board to stay compact on the handle. I taught myself the circuit design and layout for this from component datasheets, designed a printed case to mount it to the racket, and used the data to analyze my own strokes.

  • 1000 Hzsample rate
  • 3-axisaccel + impact

Quant research · Python 2026 – present

Options dealer positioning and 0DTE strategy research

Independent research

I wanted to know whether the engineering habit of distrusting any result you can't reproduce would survive contact with markets. I built a Python tool that computes dealer gamma (GEX), vanna, and charm exposure, zero-gamma flip levels, and call/put walls from option open interest using Black-Scholes Greeks, with strike-by-time heatmap dashboards and alerts running on a cloud VM. Then I backtested GEX-gated 0DTE SPY breakout strategies over 268 trading days on real NBBO option quotes against matched-null controls.

The most useful thing I found was my own mistakes. Twice a strategy looked profitable until an audit turned up lookahead bias: a confirmation signal that peeked four minutes ahead, and an exit-fill convention that priced stops before they were knowable. Fixed, a 63% win rate became 36% and +1.7% per trade became −5.5%, and live paper-trading fills agreed with the corrected numbers. After that I built a four-stage validator (deflated Sharpe, cross-instrument replication, embargoed walk-forward, sealed holdout). It has rejected all 202 hypotheses I've tested, including a SPY mean-reversion study against a rolling-OLS cross-market fair value. I count the clean negative results as the output.

  • 202hypotheses tested, 0 survivors
  • 2lookahead bugs caught in self-audit
  • 268trading days backtested
Quant · Pine Script 2026

Intraday volume-anomaly signal

A 1-minute SPY/QQQ indicator that flags six Wyckoff volume patterns by scoring each bar's volume, range, and wicks against trailing baselines (ratio or z-score). It is leak-free by construction: every reference statistic is lagged one bar, signals confirm only at bar close, and the documented fill rule is the next bar's open. Along the way I found and fixed a session-gap artifact where pre-market volume made the signal fire almost every morning at the open.

  • 6patterns
  • 0lookahead
